Alzheimer's classification from fMRI

Sept 2025 - Present • with Prof. Pablo Robles Granda

I'm building a pipeline that takes raw fMRI brain scans and turns them into functional connectivity graphs, basically a graph where each node is a brain region and the edges are how correlated their activity is. Then I train Graph Convolutional Networks on those graphs to classify Alzheimer's.

A big part of it is checking how much the choice of connectivity measure matters. I'm comparing Pearson, Spearman and Kendall correlation to see which one gives the model the most useful graph.

Predicting student well-being (StudentLife)

Sept 2025 - Present • with Prof. Pablo Robles Granda

StudentLife is a dataset of phone sensor data (activity, sleep, location, etc.) collected from college students over a term. I built a pipeline that cuts the continuous sensor streams into sliding-window sequences and trains LSTMs on them to predict students' well-being and academic outcomes.
